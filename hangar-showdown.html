COMPETITIVE MODE GUIDE

Hangar Showdown: Build a Squad That Adapts

Plan a balanced five-mech roster, manage matchups, and avoid spending your strongest options before you need them.

Launch the interactive Hangar Optimizer →

The optimizer is on the homepage and uses rule-based tactical recommendations—not live opponent scouting or verified win-rate predictions. Confirm current mode rules and equipment restrictions in-game.

1. Build five complementary roles

A balanced hangar should be able to engage at different ranges and recover when a preferred plan is countered. Avoid choosing five mechs that all need the same narrow corridor or close-range engagement.

RoleWhy it mattersPractical consideration
Entry / pressureStarts fights and forces movementNeeds a safe approach route
Area denialControls a lane or objectiveWatch for flanking paths
Long-range controlPunishes exposed opponentsRequires clear sightlines
DisruptionInterrupts enemy timingCoordinate ability use
Flexible reserveResponds to unknown matchupsKeep multiple ranges viable

2. Choose rounds based on information

  1. Opening: Favor a reliable, adaptable option while learning how opponents move.
  2. After a loss: Identify whether you lost to range, positioning, or ability timing before changing your approach.
  3. When ahead: Avoid unnecessary risk; preserve cover and force enemies to expose themselves.
  4. Deciding round: Use the strongest remaining answer to the opponent's demonstrated playstyle.

3. Coordinate with your partner

Agree on a focus target and a fallback location. Two players attacking different targets often lose trades they could win together. Keep overlapping lines of support without standing close enough to be punished by the same area attack.

4. Common mistakes

  • Bringing only short-range weapons into a map with exposed approaches.
  • Using disruption abilities before your teammate is in position to capitalize.
  • Repeating a failed rush without changing the route or timing.
  • Treating the hangar score as a guaranteed match outcome.

Quick pre-match checklist

Check weapon energy compatibility, map sightlines, cooldown coordination, and whether your five choices can cover both close and long engagements.
