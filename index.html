Advanced Winning Strategies

10-minute daily practice routine

  1. 2 min: Inspect cover, exposed lanes and safe retreat routes in a Custom Match.
  2. 3 min: Practice moving from cover to cover while keeping an exit available.
  3. 3 min: Play one match focused on your selected mode's win condition, not personal damage.
  4. 2 min: Review one avoidable death and one rotation you could improve.

Track progress using objective captures, unnecessary deaths, safe escapes and team contribution rather than kills alone.

SPECIAL MODE PLAYBOOK

Hangar Showdown: Win the Best-of-Five

Official mode rules: 2v2, up to five 90-second rounds, first team to three round wins. You must choose a different Mech every round. Equipment is maxed for battle, subject to league-specific restrictions. Verify updates in-game.

MATCH FORMAT

Think five rounds ahead

Bring a varied hangar. Plan a dependable opening pick, a close-range response, a safe midrange option, and a reserve counter for late rounds. Coordinate with your teammate rather than duplicating the same weakness.

LEAGUE RULES

Know your equipment limits

Leagues 4 and 3 disable Pilots, Implants and Mods; League 2 enables Pilots and Implants; League 1 allows all three. Mech rarity and rank affect your Showdown League. Avoid assuming your normal loadout behaves identically.

DAILY REWARDS

Use your scoring matches

The first 10 daily matches contribute Battle Points. Further matches can still award Ranking Points and XP. Prioritize consistency, thoughtful team play, and a balanced hangar over risky experiments during scoring matches.

ENTRY

Unlock requirements

Reach Division 7 and have at least four Mechs in your Hangar. Matchmaking uses a separate Hangar Showdown League, so consider the whole squad when upgrading.

Between-round checklist

  1. Check which of your Mechs remain available.
  2. Identify the opponent's threat: rush, range, or area denial.
  3. Choose a complementary role to your teammate.
  4. Take a safe first angle; avoid isolated 2v1 engagements.
  5. After the round, adjust your next pick to the map and remaining options.

Strategy is editorial advice, not a guarantee of results. Read official Hangar Showdown rules.

ARENA FIELD MANUAL

Map Guides & Positioning

Map names and broad characteristics are based on official game descriptions. The playbook recommendations are situational; sightlines, spawns and map layouts should be verified in-game. These are not literal map diagrams.

Universal map-reading checklist

Before contact

Locate two cover options, the likely enemy approach and a safe fallback route.

After a kill

Recheck the minimap. Rotate only when the objective or next engagement justifies exposure.

When losing

Avoid repeating the same exposed route. Regroup, trade angles and contest a more defensible position.

HIGHER-LEVEL SKILLS

Counterplay & Team Coordination

POSITIONING

Crossfire without overextending

Hold a different angle from an ally so one enemy cannot safely face both of you. Stay close enough to support a retreat.

SURVIVAL

Cooldown discipline

Do not spend your movement or defensive ability merely to arrive first. Save it for a favorable engagement or escape when possible.

TARGET SELECTION

Finish vulnerable targets

Prioritize isolated or exposed opponents that can be eliminated safely. Do not chase a low-health mech into multiple enemies.

COUNTERPLAY

Against long-range pressure

Break line of sight, use alternate covered routes and coordinate a flank. Avoid crossing an open lane one at a time.

COUNTERPLAY

Against close-range rushes

Keep distance, maintain an escape route and force opponents to approach through predictable cover gaps.

TEAMWORK

Call the next objective

Use concise calls: target, lane and intent. Agree on who anchors, who contests and who covers the rotation.
