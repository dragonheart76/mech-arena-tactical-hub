Better Mech Builds Start With a Role

A good weapon is not automatically a good match for every mech. Match your mech's ability, safe engagement range, and available energy before investing.

Check compatibility before committing

  1. Confirm your mech's current energy capacity.
  2. Add the energy cost of every equipped weapon and confirm the total fits.
  3. Check effective range against the mech's movement and ability profile.
  4. Test the build in multiple maps before spending scarce resources.

Upgrade priorities

Improve a small, versatile roster first. Avoid buying a weapon solely because it performs well on a different mech. Consider whether your next upgrade solves a specific recurring weakness.

Build mistakes to avoid

  • Pairing a short-range weapon with a mech that cannot safely close distance.
  • Choosing weapons with incompatible firing rhythms or ranges.
  • Ignoring reload windows and the need to retreat.
  • Upgrading every new item instead of completing reliable loadouts.
