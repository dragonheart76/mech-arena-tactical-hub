How recommendations work

The bot reads mechs.json and weapons.json from this site, scores weapon families by your mech's role, preferred range and playstyle, and recommends two complementary options. It never invents weapon energy values or game damage statistics.

Weapon families may have several energy variants. Confirm actual available variants and ability interactions in-game before spending resources. The catalog may not include every current release.
